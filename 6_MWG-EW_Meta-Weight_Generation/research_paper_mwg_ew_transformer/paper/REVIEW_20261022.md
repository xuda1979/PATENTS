## Added results

1. **Output-subspace obstruction:** every FFN output lies in the final factor's rowspace. Orthogonal target residual is unavoidable. The optimal rank-r target-output singular-value tail is a further lower bound. These are applications of classical linear algebra, not a claim of priority for a general theorem.
2. **Constant-core diagnostic:** for frozen single-projection a, V and z, the best calibration constant solves Hc=b with H=E[diag(a)VVᵀdiag(a)]. It folds into a factor. This does not imply a jointly optimal three-projection replacement or held-out improvement.
3. **Descriptor-only byte bound:** under matched schedules with a single cold factor read, materialized/fused scheduled-byte ratio ≤1+2nb_z/((d+m)b_w). It is not a physical HBM or latency bound.
4. **Fresh CPU evidence:** actual causal `LowRankFFN` residual stacks at depth 1/2/4, three seeds each, retain 53,248/106,496/212,992 bytes eagerly versus 4,096/8,192/16,384 checkpointed (13×). All outputs, losses, input gradients and parameter gradients match exactly and are finite. Conditioning is active. Random initialized synthetic fixtures; no trained-model-quality, hardware-speed or allocator-peak claim.
5. **Synthetic algebra verification:** 50 float64 fixtures; rowspace residual ≤1.42e−14, orthogonal decomposition relative residual ≤8.88e−16, constant-core versus independently constructed least squares ≤3.67e−15.

## Statistical interpretation

Canonical r384 generated-minus-persistent mean is −0.0007545, with descriptive t interval [−0.001363, −0.000146]. The old “not significant” statement was arithmetically incorrect under an ordinary unadjusted paired t calculation. We report intervals descriptively because four seed summaries do not validate the distributional assumptions, population generalization, or multiple-comparison inference.

Generated r384 is 1.95% worse in relative MSE than persistent r401 at near-matched reconstructed storage. Same-rank differences and cross-rank differences must not be conflated. The h=192 storage assumption comes from code defaults, not a complete resolved run manifest.
