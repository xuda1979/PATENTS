\begin{abstract}
We study \emph{parameter lifetime} as a systems variable in Transformer
inference. Meta-Weight Generation for On-the-Fly Ephemeral Weights (\mwg)
pairs persistent low-rank factors $U\in\R^{d_{\rm in}\times r}$,
$V\in\R^{r\times d_{\rm out}}$ with a small per-token generated diagonal
descriptor $z\in\R^{r}$ whose effective projection
$P(x;U,V,z)=((xU)\odot z)V\in\R^{d_{\rm out}}$ is produced and consumed
inside a fused kernel, never written to HBM---a \emph{no-escape/no-store}
contract that is inference-scoped. On an Ascend~910B2, the fused no-store
kernel reaches parity with the materialized arm at L2-resident geometry
($T{=}128$) and is verified algebraically equivalent. Separately, on the
training side (where the core is recomputed, not stored), the low-rank
parameterization itself makes a synchronized DDP
training step run $3.37\times$--$4.14\times$ faster than dense at 8B-scale
geometry---a rank effect, not a no-store effect. Causal FFN-layer reconstruction, measured on four disjoint
hash-bound seeds from a single coherent run family, reaches same-rank parity
with a trained persistent low-rank factor at r384, r401, and r512, with a
$+1.95\%$ relative-MSE penalty at the near-equal-budget pair; two controls
(shuffled context, rank-512 persistent factor) show most of the measured
reconstruction benefit comes from the trainable correction, not from
context-conditioned generation. Three limitations are explicit: (1)~the
operational router that skips dense-weight fetches for unselected branches is
proposed but unrealized; (2)~at beyond-L2 geometry ($T{=}4096$) the
single-core-epilogue v1 kernel runs $1.93\times$ slower than materialization,
but the measured v4 multi-core rewrite is algebraically identical to v1
($\max|\Delta|{=}0$) and $1.13\times$--$3.03\times$ faster across
$T{=}128$--$4096$, resolving the bottleneck at this geometry; (3)~cached
autoregressive
decode ($S{=}1$), continuous batching, and full-model perplexity are
unvalidated. Evidence is confined to a single FFN geometry on one device
family; we separate what is measured, what is designed but unmeasured,
and what was withdrawn, and we treat the operational router as future
work.
\end{abstract}

\subsubsection*{Reproducibility statement}
Every measured number in this paper is labeled with its provenance in
Table~\ref{tab:evidencemap}. The causal prototype is covered by a local unit-test
suite (prefix invariance, batch invariance, algebraic equivalence, controller
width, legacy collapse, checkpoint round trip). The fused no-store arm is
produced by a single audited Ascend-C source file bound to a SHA-256
source-plus-binary contract manifest; the harness re-verifies the manifest and
an algebraic-equivalence gate ($\mathrm{rtol}=\mathrm{atol}=5\times10^{-3}$) before
timing, and profiler passes are matched across arms. The analytic break-even
curves of Table~\ref{tab:regimecall} are closed-form functions of the declared
geometry and are trivially re-derivable. Appendix~\ref{app:audit-provenance}
lists every historical artifact that could not be reconstructed and is therefore
excluded from the evidence base. The benchmark harness, kernel source, contract
manifest generator, test suite, and per-run JSON artifacts (with SHA-256 hashes)
are included in the supplementary material.

\subsubsection*{Broader impact statement}
This work proposes an inference-scoped systems interface and a measurement
methodology for runtime-generated, scratch-resident neural-network operands.
The intended effect is to reduce weight-memory bandwidth and distributed
gradient communication in large-model inference and training, and we do not
anticipate direct societal harms beyond those of machine-learning serving
systems generally; potential benefits center on lower memory-bandwidth and
energy demands for large-model deployment. We deliberately
state the current evidence boundary---mechanism verified on device, causal
FFN-layer reconstruction measured at matched rank, net efficiency benefit at
scale not yet
established---so that the proposal is
not presented as a completed deployment method. Causal FFN-layer
reconstruction is measured
(same-rank parity with the persistent control at r384, r401, and r512;
a shuffled-context control bounds the context-conditioning benefit at
${\sim}0.05\%$ of MSE at r384/r401, not detectable at r512, and the
near-equal-budget comparison carries a $+1.95\%$ penalty for the generated
arm); applications that depend on strict latency, memory, or energy
guarantees should treat the
under-scaled regimes, the not-yet-measured off-chip savings at scale, and
branch-skipping dispatch reported here as active limitations rather than
supported properties.
